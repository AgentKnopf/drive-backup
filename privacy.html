Privacy policy

Drive Backup is used by one person only, its owner, on their own Google account.

  • It requests only the drive.file scope: it can see and change only files it created itself, nothing else in the Drive.
  • It uploads the owner's own files to the owner's own Drive. No data is sent to, stored by, or shared with any third party.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no advertising.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Last updated: November 2026.
